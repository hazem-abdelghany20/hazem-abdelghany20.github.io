*This book borrows openly. These are the shoulders — go read the originals; every one of them is better than my compression of it.*

**Flow** — Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi *(psychic entropy, order in consciousness, flow as the mechanism of complexity — chapters 2, 6, 23)* · **Man's Search for Meaning** — Viktor Frankl *(the last freedom; meaning through work, love, and suffering borne well — chapter 19)* · **Atomic Habits** — James Clear *(systems over goals; votes for identity — chapter 8)* · **Deep Work** & **Digital Minimalism** & **So Good They Can't Ignore You** — Cal Newport *(depth, solitude deprivation, career capital — chapters 5, 7, 12)* · **Dopamine Nation** — Anna Lembke *(the pleasure–pain balance — chapter 3)* · **How Emotions Are Made** — Lisa Feldman Barrett *(constructed emotion — chapter 4)* · **Peak** — Anders Ericsson *(deliberate practice — chapter 10)* · **Mastery** — Robert Greene *(the apprenticeship — chapter 13)* · **Range** — David Epstein *(sampling and match quality — chapter 13)* · **Thinking in Bets** — Annie Duke *(resulting; decision vs. outcome — chapter 14)* · **Fooled by Randomness** — Nassim Taleb *(survivorship bias — chapter 17)* · **The Success Equation** — Michael Mauboussin *(the luck–skill continuum — chapter 17)* · **Four Thousand Weeks** — Oliver Burkeman *(finitude — chapters 24, 25)* · **The Comfort Crisis** — Michael Easter *(discomfort as nutrient — chapters 1, prologue)* · **Meditations** — Marcus Aurelius *(the private ledger of a working commander — everywhere)* · **Al-Hikam** — Ibn Ata'illah al-Iskandari *(the aphorisms on decree and striving that shaped chapters 16 and 25)* — and beneath all of it, the Qur'an and the Sunnah, from which the load-bearing ideas of intention, sabr, shukr, rizq, and tawakkul are drawn, imperfectly, by an author who is still learning them.
